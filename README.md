# Kent Test Practice

A small, mobile-first revision app for the Kent Test reasoning paper.

## What's in it

Four verbal and two non-verbal reasoning question types plus a timed drill. Every question is marked straight away with an explanation of the method.

- **Alphabet Codes** (verbal 3): questions generated on the fly with auto-written explanations and a number-line picture of the jumps. Easy, Mixed and Hard.
- **Odd Two Out** (verbal 4): a bank of 69 hand-written questions, each with the reason the three go together and why the two do not. "Tricky ones only" draws the 27 with a trap.
- **Compound Words** (verbal 2): a bank of 83 hand-written questions. Pick one word from each group. "Tricky ones only" draws the 35 where the sound changes or the halves are not words.
- **One Letter, Four Words** (verbal 1): a bank of 62 hand-written questions with the four words spelled out.
- **Shape Sequences** (non-verbal 2): generated on the fly. One or two properties of a shape (outline, fill, size, turn, corner marker, dot count) change by a rule across five frames; find the missing one. Explanations walk each rule.
- **Shape Transformations** (non-verbal 3): generated on the fly. A changes to B by one or two changes (turn, mirror, fill, size, dots); apply the same to C.
- **Timed drill**: 10 questions mixed from every type, 30 seconds each counting down. At zero the question is marked and revealed. No hints. Built to stop him leaving blanks.
- Three hints per practice session. A hint reveals half the working, never the answer.
- Per-question timer against the 30-second guide, and an all-time score per type kept in the browser.
